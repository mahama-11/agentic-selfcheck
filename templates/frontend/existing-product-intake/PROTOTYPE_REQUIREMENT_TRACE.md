## Coverage rules

- Every major requirement must map to at least one prototype surface or explicit out-of-scope decision.
- Every major visible function must map to backend/API feasibility.
- Every surfaced state must have screenshot or artifact evidence before prototype review.
- Missing support must be documented internally and translated out of user-facing UI.

## Non-regression rule

For existing-product iterations, do not regress current working product paths unless the tradeoff is explicitly accepted by the human reviewer.
